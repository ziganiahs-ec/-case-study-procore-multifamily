# Procore Implementation & Document Control – Esplanade at Aventura, Fl
## 🏗 Project Overview
- Esplanade at Biscayne is an eight-story, 402-unit multifamily residential development located at 11150 Biscayne Boulevard in the North Miami area. During active construction phases, the project team required structured Procore administration, reliable document control, and improved workflow visibility across stakeholders.
 
---

## 👤 My Role
Project Coordinator / Procore Administrator

I supported the project team by administering and configuring Procore workflows, managing construction documentation, and strengthening reporting visibility across the project lifecycle.

---

## 🎯 Challenge
The team needed:

- Consistent Procore adoption across team members
- Training in Procore to the Field team including the Project Manager and Assistant Project Manager 
- Structured tracking of RFIs and submittals
- Creeation of Daily Logs, including records of photos, equipment inventory,  
- Reliable document control processes  
- Improved visibility into project communications, distribution list
- Alignment between field teams and office stakeholders
- Timely information, inmediate knowlege of the approval  responses of the External Teams  

Without disciplined workflows, there was risk of delayed responses and fragmented documentation.

---

## 🚀 Actions & Contributions

- Configured and maintained Procore project structure and permissions  
- Administered high-volume RFI and submittal workflows  
- Standardized document control procedures across the team  
- Supported onboarding of project stakeholders into Procore  
- Coordinated drawing and specification updates  
- Strengthened reporting visibility for project leadership  
- Facilitated communication between field, consultants, and office teams 

---

## 📊 Results & Impact

- Improved consistency and organization of project documentation  
- Increased visibility into RFI and submittal status  
- Reduced communication gaps across stakeholders  
- Strengthened team adoption of Procore workflows  
- Enhanced overall project controls discipline
